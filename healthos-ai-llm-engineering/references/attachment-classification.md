## Explicitly out of scope or delegated

The attachment does not authorize this skill to absorb platform implementation, persistence, backend architecture, full security governance, overall QA, CI/CD, production observability architecture, health-platform acquisition, or clinical meaning. Those remain delegated to the specialist skills listed in `references/boundaries.md`. The AI skill owns the AI-specific contract, controls, telemetry fields, tests, and routing at those boundaries.

No material requirement was classified as contradictory. The only potentially conflicting request was to make the skill “final” while also preserving universal reuse; this integration resolves that by keeping provider-neutral and reusable core guidance, with HealthOS-specific safety overlays and provider references loaded only when relevant.
