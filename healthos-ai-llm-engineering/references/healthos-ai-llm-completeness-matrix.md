## Acceptance interpretation

`PASS` means the requirement has an explicit substantive home, one authoritative owner, evidence, and a verification route. `CONTROLLED` means currentness or profile applicability is intentionally implementation-time and must be re-checked; it is not a frozen provider or domain guarantee. Product-specific runtime safety, privacy, regulatory, contractual, and domain approvals remain required before deployment.
