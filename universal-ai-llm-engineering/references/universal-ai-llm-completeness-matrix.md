## Acceptance interpretation

`VERIFIED` means the requirement has an explicit substantive home, one authoritative owner, a concrete file and section, an identified source, evidence, and an executed verification route. `CONTROLLED` means currentness or profile applicability is intentionally implementation-time and must be re-checked; it is not a frozen provider or domain guarantee. `PRESENT`, `COMPLETE`, and `CORRECT` are evidence-gated claims, not text-existence claims. Product-specific runtime safety, privacy, regulatory, contractual, and domain approvals remain required before deployment.

The hardening audit preserves all prior requirement rows and adds explicit file, section, source, evidence, verification-status, and currentness-status fields required by the final audit instructions.
